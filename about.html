About Maheshwari Co.

Four decades of excellence as India's premier industrial aluminium distributor and stockist.

Established 1978

Bridging Primary Manufacturers and High-Tech Industries

Maheshwari Co. stands as an authoritative stockist and distributor for Hindalco Industries Ltd. and NALCO. We specialize in catering to multi-sectoral industrial demands including automotive tooling, defense equipment, architectural facades, electrical distribution, and HVAC manufacturing.

With central warehousing facilities and dedicated staff, we provide seamless Pan-India logistical coverage with guaranteed mill test certificates with quality verification.

Our founder Mr. B.K. Bhuraria, launched aluminium distribution in 1966 under the name of M/s Bhuraria Brothers and was appointed the authorised distributors for Hindalco Industries Ltd. In 1978, a new company was formed by the name of Maheshwari Co.which went on to become one of the largest distributors of Aluminium Semi-fabricated Products in India.

Mr. Bhuraria is also the president of Aluminium Association Of Delhi for the last 10 years. In 1993, Mr. Amit Bhuraria joined the aluminium business and has been looking into the procurement and marketing of metals. Ms. Arushi Bhuraria is presently serving as the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) of Maheshwari Company.
