Understanding 6061 vs 6063 Aluminium Alloys

Published: September 2026 | Reading Time: 6 mins

When selecting extruded aluminium sections or plates, choosing between 6061 and 6063 depends on whether your project requires high structural load strength (6061-T6) or superior anodized architectural finishes (6063-T5/T6).
